- The document alternates between proprietorship/small-enterprise language and the phrase "founding partners." The page calls the three people founders in the company account and does not state a legal partnership or director structure.

- The people in the supplied photographs are not matched to founder names or formal roles.
- Claims about certifications, production volumes, export reach, specific factory equipment, client scale, artificial-additive exclusions across the whole portfolio and current availability are not published.
- Golden Pearl process details are kept general because no batch record or approved process photography was supplied.

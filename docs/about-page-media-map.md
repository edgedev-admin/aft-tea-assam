## Editorial Facts Requiring Company Confirmation

- Which named partners, if any, are father and son.
- The individual contribution, title and chronology attached to each named partner.
- The names of the people shown in the family photographs and approval to publish those names.
- Any first-person recollections intended for a future family-legacy chapter.
- A current grower or supplier count. The approximately 220-grower figure is from a 2022 report and is not published as a present-day total.

Until these points are confirmed, the public page tells the family story collectively and does not publish the proposed father-and-son headline or individual biographies.

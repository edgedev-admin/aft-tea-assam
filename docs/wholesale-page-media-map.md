## Art direction decision

No verified AFT manufacturing, bulk packing, grade, or cupping photography is currently available in the repository. The wholesale hero and commercial capability sections are therefore typography-led. Generic serving photographs and consumer pack shots were not presented as evidence of wholesale operations.

## Requested first-party media

1. Fresh-leaf intake and inspection at the current Dikom or Lahowal operation.
2. Current CTC grades photographed consistently at close range, with grade labels approved by the team.
3. Nahorchukbari whole-leaf and golden-tip selections.
4. A real grading and cupping setup showing dry leaf, infused leaf and liquor.
5. Verified bulk packaging and dispatch preparation.
6. Current private-label or packed supply examples that AFT has permission to publish.

Record capture date, location, ownership, consent, depicted process and current-operational status before publishing future media.

## Missing authentic asset requirements

The typography-led sections should be replaced or supplemented only when traceable AFT media is supplied. Preferred captures:

1. Fresh-leaf intake and inspection at Dikom or Lahowal, including a wide environmental frame and a close leaf detail.
2. Withering troughs in operation, captured without staging unsafe access.
3. Specialty rolling or hand-finishing with the actual team and current equipment.
4. CTC machinery and output from the current production line.
5. Sorting and grade comparison with labels confirmed by the AFT team.
6. A real cupping table showing dry leaf, infused leaf and liquor evaluation.
7. Current packing and dispatch preparation for retail and wholesale supply.

For every future asset, record capture date, location, photographer/owner, consent, depicted process, and whether it represents current operations. Do not caption a regional or stock image as an AFT facility.

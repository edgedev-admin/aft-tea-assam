# Our Teas page verification notes

## Content and naming

- `High Societea` is the public product name used on the supplied photographed pack and on the existing product detail route.
- The company document uses `Ajanta Societea`. The relationship between those names is not confirmed, so the collection page preserves `High Societea` and does not publish a combined or alternate product name.
- `Ajanta Elaichi`, `Golden Needles & Velvet Tips`, the orthodox grades, Ekoratoli grades, chocolate tea and seasonal releases are supported by the company-provided document. Current formulations, lots and availability remain enquiry-led.
- No definitive ingredient list is published for Kolkata Street Masala Chai because a current ingredient panel has not been supplied.
- The Golden Pearl auction figure remains on its dedicated detail page with date and historic-result context. The collection page avoids presenting the result as a current price.

## Media policy

- The `/teas` collection page intentionally renders no existing Ajanta package photography, hidden image elements, placeholders or generated product artwork.
- `TeaCatalogProduct.media` supports optional product, campaign and lifestyle media with desktop/mobile sources, intrinsic dimensions, alt text and object position when approved assets become available.
- No media field is populated in the current collection catalog, so the page issues no product-image requests.

## Editorial boundaries

- CTC is explained once in the Ekoratoli chapter.
- Availability language is deliberately enquiry-led and does not imply live stock, pricing or ecommerce.
- Existing product detail slugs are preserved for Ajanta Gold, High Societea, Kolkata Street Masala Chai and Golden Pearl.

### Our Beginning

AFT Techno Trade was established in 2018 by Noor Alam Khan, Imran Khan and Aslam Khan. From Dikom and Lahowal in Dibrugarh district, they set out to combine practical tea-making knowledge with a belief that Upper Assam leaf could serve both the everyday market and the exacting world of handcrafted specialty tea.

### Why Upper Assam

Dikom and Lahowal sit within the Brahmaputra valley's tea-growing landscape. Warm, humid conditions, seasonal rainfall and proximity to cultivation give the region its recognised style: robust body, briskness, malty depth and rich colour. Keeping processing close to the source helps make fresh-leaf handling a central part of the work.

## Craft (/craft)

### SEO

- **Title:** Tea Manufacturing & Craft
- **Description:** Follow AFT Techno Trade's tea process from fresh-leaf selection and withering to specialty or CTC production, grading, cupping and dispatch.

### Hero

- **H1:** The craft behind every leaf.
- **Body:** From fresh-leaf selection to processing, grading and tasting, tea quality is shaped by careful decisions at every stage.

### Process

1. **Leaf Selection:** Fresh-leaf intake begins with visual inspection. Tender shoots, including the bud and young leaves, are prioritised while coarse or damaged leaf can be separated.
2. **Withering:** Air moves through the leaf to reduce moisture and make it pliable. The degree of wither is adjusted to the tea style being made.
3. **Specialty or CTC:** Whole-leaf specialty lots use gentler rolling and handling. CTC production crushes, tears and curls the leaf into small, quick-infusing granules.
4. **Oxidation & Drying:** Time, temperature, moisture, colour and aroma guide oxidation. Drying then stabilises the tea and preserves its finished character.
5. **Sorting & Grading:** Dried tea is separated by particle size and style, including CTC grades such as BP, PF and PD and orthodox whole-leaf grades.
6. **Cupping:** Samples are assessed for dry leaf, infused leaf, liquor colour, brightness, body, aroma and finish before a lot is prepared for supply.
7. **Packaging & Dispatch:** Tea is protected from moisture and prepared for retail or commercial movement. Current formats and quantities are agreed for each order.

### Cupping Criteria

Dry leaf, infused leaf, liquor, body and mouthfeel, aroma and finish.

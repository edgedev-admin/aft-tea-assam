## Operations and Wholesale

The website uses qualified language for leaf inspection, withering, specialty versus CTC processing, oxidation, drying, grading, cupping, moisture protection and dispatch. These are company-provided operational descriptions, not an external factory audit.

The following require commercial confirmation for each enquiry and are not promised as fixed public terms:

- Minimum order quantities.
- Exact bulk bag sizes, including the document's 20–50 kg range.
- Lead times and dispatch schedules.
- Sample policy.
- Exact technical specifications or certificates of analysis.
- Private-label packaging formats.
- “Identical” seasonal cup profiles.
- Nationwide availability or named customer categories as proof of current supply.
- Current pricing, margins or retailer support.

## Claims Excluded or Softened

- “Fully integrated estate-level facility.”
- “No intermediaries” and absolute farm-direct supply claims.
- “100% authentic” or “never blend off-origin tea” as universal guarantees.
- “Single-estate certifications.”
- Sustainability, fair-payment or social-impact claims without evidence.
- Food-safety certifications or compliance marks not supplied.
- Exact moisture reduction and finished-moisture percentages as public guarantees.
- Exact chemical composition claims for AFT teas.
- Health, antioxidant or wellness benefits.
- “World-class,” “national benchmark,” “manufacturing powerhouse” and similar promotional superlatives.
- Claims that all products contain only tea; the documented range includes spices and flavour concepts.

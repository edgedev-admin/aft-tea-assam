# Craft Page Content Verification

## Sources reviewed

- `AFT TECHNO TRADE DETAILS .docx`, supplied by the project owner.
- Existing site product and company copy in `/about`, `/teas`, and `/wholesale`.
- The Our Craft 2.0 implementation brief.

## Claims used

- AFT Techno Trade works in Dikom and Lahowal near Dibrugarh in Upper Assam.
- The broad workflow covers leaf assessment, withering, processing, oxidation and drying where appropriate, sorting, grading, cupping, packaging and dispatch.
- The portfolio includes Nahorchukbari specialty teas and Ekoratoli CTC teas.
- BP, PF and PD are relevant CTC grades; selected orthodox teas use separate leaf classifications.
- Commercial preparation can vary between retail and bulk requirements.

## Deliberate qualifications

- The seven stages are described as a broad journey, not one universal recipe.
- Oxidation and handling are explicitly described as tea-style dependent.
- Cupping is presented as sensory evaluation, not as a laboratory certification.
- The Assam landscape image is described as regional context and is not represented as an AFT-owned estate or facility.

## Claims intentionally omitted

- Exact moisture percentages, temperatures, timings, capacities or machinery lists.
- Universal quality guarantees, certification claims, purity claims or shelf-life claims.
- Statements that every specialty tea follows the same oxidation or production method.
- Any claim that the available generic tea imagery documents AFT operations.

These details require current operating records, certification evidence, or verified first-party photography before publication.
